title = "Lickety-Sync"
+++

Lickety-Sync syncs folders between your own devices and the people you share
with, and contacts no server you did not turn on. No accounts. No tracker. Every
connection is encrypted, and a folder syncs only with devices that hold one of
its keys: one to change it, one to read it, and one to store it without being
able to read it.

**Status:** in development; nothing to download yet. The source and the design
specifications are on [GitHub](https://github.com/LicketySync).
