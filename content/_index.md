title = "Lickety-Sync"
+++

Lickety-Sync syncs folders between your own devices and the people you share
with, and never contacts a server. No accounts. No tracker. Every connection is
encrypted, and every key is one you issued and can revoke.

**Status:** in development; nothing to download yet. The plan and the source are
on [GitHub](https://github.com/licketysync).
